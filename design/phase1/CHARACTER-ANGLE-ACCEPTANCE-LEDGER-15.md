## New N/back view — accepted as independent visual candidate
The one-person, no-label BACK image is a distinct 1086×1448 original: tousled dark brown hair, gold-stitching teal Wayline cloak with large dorsal compass motif, warm brass lantern hanging from Tavi's physical RIGHT hand (picture-right from behind), leather map satchel at physical LEFT hip (picture-left from behind), and same heavy boots. The cape's intentionally unequal hem and travel-worn materials remain identifiable.

**Remaining critiques:**
- Original rear compass pattern differs in exact ray lengths and path arrangement from the earlier front/right embroidery, and garment folding changes visibility. An original **reference pattern** `design/phase1/tavi-rear-wayline-cloak-embroidery-v7.svg` was committed to specify future dorsal topology; it is a vector art guide, not evidence that the new painting already matches every stitch.
- Proportions appear coherent but no shared-character 3D model or physically measured height/shoulder rig has been made. The paintings have comparable 1086×1448 source canvases, not guaranteed equal anatomical scale in world units.
- Rear cloak obscures some satchel strap geometry; 3D attachment needs later authorized validation. The bag *is* on the correct side in the visible drawing.
- Facial detail cannot validate rear identity; rely on coat, boot and hair silhouettes.
