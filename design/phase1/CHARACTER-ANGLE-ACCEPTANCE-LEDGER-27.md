## Story canon and visual continuity

Source [story bible](../../docs/02-NARRATIVE-AND-CHARACTERS.md) governs:
- **Nella** is Tavi's impulsive, quick-witted childhood friend. After Brasshollow she plays an independent role in reconstruction; never silently assigned healer, tinkerer, permanent playable party class or Echo Lantern. The new four images preserve copper hair, olive coat, ochre scarf, practical pouch, patched trousers and laced boots.
- **Orin** is Tavi's **elderly human grandfather**, retired road surveyor and Echo Lantern guardian. He starts seated repairing maps, departs to help others; he is NOT a dead mentor, dwarf, armored warrior or hidden villain. The portrait uses a mundane survey staff/tool and aged travel garments; this is a candidate costume, not a locked technical prop.
- **Seren Vale** is the **human last High Bellkeeper**, morally accountable antagonist driven by fear of another Severance. Her solemn restrained bellkeeper silhouette is appropriate; no pointed ears, possession or elf sorcery.

**Art quality note:** Orin and Seren, and current Nella, appear much more naturalistically proportioned than the first original youthful, larger-headed Tavi FRONT. A consistent **premium but achievable 2.5D in-game silhouette and anatomy** is not yet demonstrated. Nella's face and sash/coat construction still differ between independently produced angles. G1 stays OPEN until deliberately normalized.
