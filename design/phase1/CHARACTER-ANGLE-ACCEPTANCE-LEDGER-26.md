## Accepted NEW premium painterly concept: Nella solo FRONT

**One actual original file:** `15_NEW_NELLA_SOLO_FRONT_PAINTING.png`, **1024×1536 RGB**.

Native studio portrait inspected as one single youthful human female figure, full-length from messy copper-red hair to two rugged boots, on one continuous warm parchment background, with **no captions, cast board, second subject, Tavi, Echo Lantern or claimed game-progress labels**.

Visual observations and tentative costume ingredients:

- Copper-red curly hair swept into a large loose top knot with long wind-tossed curls; lively eyes, freckles, playful confident expression. Expression and stance support her impulsive, quick-witted personality.
- Practical layered **olive/forest-green traveling coat**, weathered cream shirt, warm **mustard/ochre scarf**, rolled sleeves, compact strapped leather side pouch, modest belt buckles, visibly patched brown trousers and lace-up road boots. No teal Wayline/compass map cape and no Echo Lantern.
- The portrait has a clear distinct warm copper/olive silhouette that separates Nella from Tavi's teal/amber identity. Hands partly in pockets; no invented combat weapon, healing staff, fantasy species or assigned playable class.
- **Important remaining quality issue:** Nella's face/body proportions and painting treatment are somewhat more realistically mature than the oversized-eyed original youthful Tavi FRONT. Their head-to-body ratios, fine-material detail, scale and world art integration need a deliberate common 2.5D target before G1; the clean image itself does not prove the final in-game art will match it.

**Disposition:** FIRST valid native full-resolution independently painted **Nella FRONT preliminary candidate**. **Not** a full five/eight-direction Nella set, final cast costume, animation rig, PWA frame, game-ready sprite, 2.5D model or owner G1 approval.
