# WAYBELL — Single-Person Supporting Cast Portrait Contract 21

**Authority:** `docs/02-NARRATIVE-AND-CHARACTERS.md` and Phase 1 Review 21. **G1 owner approval pending, Phase 2/game code locked.**

## Why this document exists
Across multiple Phase 1 attempts, requesting an isolated Nella portrait instead yielded an ornate Tavi eight-direction overview with small supplemental portraits, decorative labels, invented role cards and simulated development statuses. Those are composition failures: a tiny inset of Nella cannot count as an independently composed, premium, native-resolution painted model. Do **not** accept another board simply because it looks polished. Each result must be one character, one native source image, and have an independently inspected story role.

## Universal hard composition
- **A single individual, full-length crown to both boots**, one pose and one neutral parchment or quiet painterly studio background; no scenic montage, room, image-in-image, title, captions, diagrams, labels, legends, checkmarks, game screenshots, party lineup or Tavi.
- **Vertical premium painterly 2.5D miniature concept aesthetic** with believable anatomy, fabric thickness, stable readable silhouette and a grounded boot-contact shadow. One primary figure fills most of the portrait without cropping feet or hair.
- Real standalone native-sized image required. Never extract or enlarge an inset from an image-generation review board and call it a full-size original.
- At each next view, supply the **accepted actual previous individual painting**, not an auto-generated marketing sheet, as a visual reference. Construct matching left/right/body parts by physical ownership, not whole-image mirroring.
- Accept/reject on actual visible image content, not instructions or on-image supposed credits, release versions or role labels.

## Nella — FRONT primary master
**Canon:** Tavi's quick-witted, impulsive **human childhood friend**, lost at the first collapse and recovered later at Brasshollow. Her life and decisions remain independent; she helps regional reconstruction. Do not silently make her a designated healer, tinkerer, blacksmith, bard, witch, elf, compulsory combat party class or generic romantic reward.

**Visual exploration tokens (not final owner-locked design):** youthful human explorer, copper-red hair, lively alert expression, olive/leaf-green practical layered travel clothing, light walking boots, compact utility pouch, scuffed knees and mobile posture. Distinct from Tavi's dark curls, teal Wayline map cloak and brass Echo Lantern; **no Echo Lantern or identical cartographer gear**. Her visual distinctiveness must survive a phone-sized silhouette even without text labels.

**Accept only:** one full-standing female-coded or age-appropriate youthful human character with visibly readable hair, ordinary journey-worn clothes and an active personality, neutral direct front view, boots fully pictured. No visual assertion that Nella is already a party character or class.

## Orin — FRONT primary master
**Canon:** Tavi's **elderly human grandfather**, former road surveyor and guardian of the Echo Lantern. Initially seated outdoors repairing charts, later travels to assist displaced communities. Not a dead mentor, metalworker/smith, armored warrior, fantasy dwarf or secret network mastermind.

**Visual exploration tokens:** visibly older human face/neck/hands, silver-grey beard/hair and age lines, practical ochre/brown wool layers, reading spectacles, bundled worn charts and surveying tape or paper. Maintain ordinary human scale—do not compress body into dwarf proportions. A personal instrument can be a survey tool, not a magical combat staff. Distinguish Orin's elderly stoop/personality without making his work a combat class.

**Accept only:** one authentic full-length elderly human portrait, not a younger rugged soldier with a beard.

## Seren Vale — FRONT primary master
**Canon:** **Human last High Bellkeeper**; her Quieting comes from real fear of centralized network catastrophe and causes morally consequential isolation. She is compassionate, controlled and accountable; not an elf, archmage, possessed villain, demon, party member or a generic boss in spiked armor.

**Visual exploration tokens:** dignified adult human, poised but burdened demeanor, restrained midnight/navy formal garments with burgundy lining and subtle aged brass bell-work; stately human silhouette, sober eye contact and modest ornament. Physical agency and inner conflict through stance rather than enormous magical weapon effects. No pointed ears, attack spells or class emblems.

**Accept only:** one full-length human Bellkeeper portrait, neutral posture and subtle wear of ceremonial duty, no fantasy elf/archmage shorthand.
