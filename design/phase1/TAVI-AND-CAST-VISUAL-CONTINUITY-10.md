## 1. Canonical physical ownership
The hero has one body and one wardrobe, regardless of facing direction. Editing/production must **not mirror the entire costume** when turning east ↔ west.
- **Tavi's physical RIGHT:** primary hand carrying the compact brass Echo Lantern while exploring. During sword use the lantern is secured at the **right hip** or a right-handed retention point, not magically duplicated. Exact holster/weapon conflict must be verified with real posing later.
- **Tavi's physical LEFT:** leather map satchel rests at the left hip, supported by a diagonal cross-body strap. A rear view of this hip may appear screen-right depending on view direction; track the actual body side, not image-side appearance.
- **Chest:** one restrained brass clasp/waybell symbol near collar; an independent belt compass remains securely attached, not floating at different heights.
- **Cloak:** a single asymmetric deep-teal cape with a gold Wayline route-map motif, same hem edge and clasp position throughout; no cape/scarf redesign between combat and exploration.
- **Hair:** short, tousled dark brown; a directional fringe silhouette identifiable at small rendered size.
- **Feet and scale:** boot height, ankle line, trouser cuff and ground contact consistent across all eight views. The design target of roughly 4.2–4.8 head heights remains **provisional**; no measured production proportions approved.

## 3. Cross-pose inventory continuity
For the four earlier vector art poses at `design/phase1/tavi-action-silhouettes-v3.svg`:
1. Exploration: lantern in right hand, sword sheathed; cape at rest.
2. Guard: sword drawn; lantern relocated to right hip with a visible retainer.
3. Sword wind-up: cape and arm silhouette must not hide swing cue; retain physical belt inventory.
4. Dodge: boot/shadow contact and head facing must remain readable; cape trails without clipping.

**Risk:** the illustrative combat painting shows sword and lantern together, but the hand/holster arrangement is not an exact production attachment solution. The issue should be solved in a matched-action character sheet before G1, not improvised when creating a game rig.

## 4. Canonical cast language and anti-drift checks
- **Nella:** copper-red hair, energetic grounded human explorer and concise field gear. More animated posture than Tavi; no healer/mage party slot, no invented class equipment, no duplicate teal cloak.
- **Orin:** older cartographer with white/gray hair and beard, spectacles, map tubes and practical wool. Maintain recognizably human adult proportions; **not** a fantasy dwarf/warrior class.
- **Seren Vale:** stern but compassionate High Bellkeeper in restrained navy/burgundy and brushed brass, with controlled upright bearing. No demonic armor or contradictory purple-pink elf/bard character. Story role is not a party combat class.
- **Tavi:** lone player-character silhouette; companions can be story-rich but not automatically depicted as three playable battle teammates.

The existing cast images and shared-scale vectors serve as references; none have a new full professional matched turnaround this pass. Reject generated review-board images claiming cast classes, heights or role swaps unsupported by the story bible.
