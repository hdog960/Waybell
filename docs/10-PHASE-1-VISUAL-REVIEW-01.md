## Candidate aesthetic: crafted high-fantasy storybook diorama
- **Shape language:** round/inhabited village roofs, carved stone bridges, larger-than-life horizon monuments, vine-covered industrial silhouettes, and strong bell/Wayline glyphs with one consistent graphic language.
- **Material language:** hand-finished timber, irregular stone blocks, cloth with visible stitching, brushed brass with readable patina, dense leaf clusters, clear water and controlled specular highlights. Art should LOOK like tactile miniature 3D models, not like watercolor pasted onto flat surfaces.
- **Lighting:** warm golden sun in Thimblebrook, physically consistent shadows and strong separation between foreground play plane and far scenery; local orange lanterns at dusk. Avoid overwrought bloom that hides gameplay objects.
- **Palette:** teal for Tavi's identifying cloak; amber brass/light as interactive magic; village foliage vivid greens and florals; sandstone/cobble grays; dark umber and wood textures; distant mist blues. Warmth should not imply all six other regions share this palette.
- **Character:** curious, young Tavi of modest adventurer proportions, short tousled dark-brown hair, distinctive teal asymmetrical cape with cartographic stitching, brass clasp, satchel for maps, practical boots, a singular recognizable Echo Lantern. Nella = red/orange hair, more energetic movement silhouette. Orin = kind older white/gray beard, map tubes and glasses.
- **Brand and world grammar:** emphasize unique Wayline markers, mapped paths, small ancient bell-stones and the Echo Lantern's warm geometric glowing memory patterns; these must differentiate WAYBELL from generic medieval fantasy art. A bell is civic/navigation infrastructure, not a generic decoration.
- **Staging:** travel edges and bridges should form visible routes through each frame, with occasional scenic payoff and purposeful navigation cues. Foreground foliage is beautiful but must never obscure the player's body or enemies when actually used in-game.

## Visual critique and status (independent assistant assessment, not user signoff)
### Strong evidence in chosen concept board
- Character/cast relationship reads immediately from the character plate; lantern and cape can be strong anchors.
- Village successfully feels like a place players want to revisit. The central lane/bridge visually anchor space.
- Reveal composition earns scale by using Tavi's small silhouette against layered landmarks.
- Mill illustrates real vertical architectural intent and puzzle opportunities.

### Required correction/validation before G1 can pass
- Illustration is not a **gameplay camera**; selected board is cinematic and sometimes too busy for iPhone. Produce a separate landscape 16:9 in-game composition with small controllable Tavi and safe left/right input zones; ensure path/ledge/portal outlines legible at phone size.
- Character has partial front/side/back studies, but not authoritative eight-direction turnaround and complete expression/tools poses. Lock one canonical costume/face before accepting character art.
- Visual designs rely too heavily on generic familiar high-fantasy village conventions; emphasize original Wayline/Bellkeeper iconography, architecture and map-symbol language.
- Mill concept shows running waterwheel and abundant waterfalls; first-visit dungeon canon is abandoned, machinery dormant. Label images as mood/design studies, not literal first-entry level.
- No Rootspool silhouette, lantern modes' visual feedback sheet, combat shot showing Tavi/enemy/boss warning against controls, or two contrasting mill interior views yet.
- No real collision, shadow, height, depth sorting, camera occlusion, actual 3D assets or mobile performance has been tested (these require Phase 2 after owner gate).
- Lightest/smallest props may blur on physical phone; do a deliberate half-screen/phone-size readability test before final visual gate.
- Two additional illustrated scenic variants introduced inconsistent protagonists (red cape/blonde companion/dog/party), and a later generated faux presentation renamed the cast; **REJECT** those images as non-canonical. Preserve Tavi, Nella, Orin, Seren, and story rules in docs/02.
