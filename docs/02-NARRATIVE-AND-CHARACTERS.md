# WAYBELL — Narrative, Cast, and Dialogue Bible
Version: Phase 0 / 1.0 | Authority: story chronology, character arcs, magic canon, endings.

## Cosmology and fixed magic rules
Waylines are impressions of repeated travel preserved in specific ancient substrate stones. Great Bells amplify and stabilize pathways and certain enchanted crossings. Most normal geography is unaffected by silence: hills, villages and stone houses do not randomly vanish. Destroyed/silenced Waylines disrupt magical road infrastructure and blur *route-associated recollections*; they do not erase a person's full identity. Echo Lantern reads imprints, not unrestricted time travel. A recovered echo cannot resurrect the dead or conjure personal truth that was never recorded. Restored roads must ultimately be maintained by living communities.

**The Great Severance:** The early Bellkeepers attempted to centralize every Wayline into a perfect permanent map. The control mechanism overloaded, destroying links and damaging collective route memories. Survivors dismantled the central hub and scattered the blueprints.

**The Quieting:** Seren, fearful of repeating the disaster, progressively suppresses the Great Bells to prevent reactivation of any old central hub. Her method would indefinitely isolate towns. She is motivated by fear and caring, not revenge.

**Resolution:** Tavi, with discovered blueprint fragments and community support, replaces mandatory centralized routing with a voluntary decentralized framework. Some old ways are irrecoverable; new roads must be made. The story ends with hope without magical undo of change.

## Character sheet and voice
**Tavi (player):** A curious, perceptive, initially sheltered apprentice mapmaker. Art direction: recognizable teal cape, satchel, compass, Echo Lantern; limited cosmetic options. Voice: thoughtful, sparing; does not quip through grief. Arc: from recording places to accepting their inevitable changes. Gender presentation and appearance options must not silently invalidate authored animations; technical selection pending. Choices change local dialogue and outcomes, not central canon.

**Nella:** Tavi's impulsive, quick-witted childhood friend. Lost in first collapse, retrieved at Brasshollow after second major dungeon. Does not become a passive rescued companion: guides regional reconstruction, challenges Tavi's reluctance to move forward. Their final separate departures affirm independent futures.

**Orin:** Elderly grandfather and former road surveyor, guardian of Echo Lantern. Starts reliably seated outdoors repairing maps. Withholds his professional association with old network mapping (not mastermind guilt). Departs in Act II to help displaced people build new settlement. Reunited late; empty chair beat is absence through meaningful relocation, not gratuitous death.

**Seren Vale:** Last High Bellkeeper, antagonist with a credible safety argument. Silences Great Bells to prevent another Severance. Confrontation forces her to recognize that forced stasis destroys connection. Remains accountable, not suddenly forgiven for harm. No villainous possession or secret demon twist.

**Pip Wren:** Traveling merchant, abandoned hometown mystery; provides inventory and regional rumors; quest in docs/03.
**Mara Flint:** Brasshollow engineer, worries about inherited technical risks; repairs lifts and helps stabilize network.
**Brother Fen:** Wilderbloom caretaker tending a sanctuary whose travelers disappeared.
**Yselle Reed:** Tidemarrow archivist, protects histories from route-linked loss.
**Dorrik Ash:** Forgekeeper torn between ancestral home and safety.
**Iven Sol:** Snowglass astronomer, discovers decentralization approach.
**The Ferryman:** Last traveler known to have crossed all original Waylines. Appears at fractured crossings and offers cryptic but fair guidance. Mystery resolved through optional journal clues, not a combat boss.

## Canonical chapter-by-chapter outline (12 chapters)
1. **Last Ordinary Morning** (Long Reach): chores, Nella exploration, first collapse, lantern; hook.
2. **Road With No Footsteps** (Long Reach -> Wilderbloom): experience first field panorama; disappearing route evidence.
3. **First Bell** (Rootbound Mill): acquire Rootspool, restore first Great Bell, detect dangerous old systems responding.
4. **City of Brass** (Brasshollow / Bellfound Choir): Seren's warning; Sunlens; rescue Nella; identify human interference.
5. **Unwritten Names** (branch setup): meet Yselle and Dorrik, explore archival and forge histories in either order.
6. **Drowned Archive** (Tidemarrow): Tideweights and first blueprint half; moral cost of Severance.
7. **Hearth Forge** (Ashglass): Ironhand and second blueprint half; old centralized apparatus design revealed.
8. **Home Is Not Still** (Long Reach): return to changed village, Orin departs, Seren reveals Quieting objective.
9. **Last Starpath** (Snowglass / Sleeping Orrery): Galecloak, Iven's decentralized routing insight.
10. **Map Made Together** (all regions): gather community support; optional prior quests alter montage and helpers.
11. **Unmade Road** (Broken Horizon): Seren begins Quieting, full-tool final traversal and final encounter.
12. **Roads We Leave** (epilogue): voluntary interconnected network; irreversible human change; Tavi, Nella depart separately, home refrain and blank map sheet.
**Ordering note:** In player state, chapters 6 and 7 can occur in either order; chapter numbering is narrative display priority, NOT a linear requirement. Chapter 8 triggers only after both blueprint halves.

## Act character transformations
Act I: Tavi leaves comfort; Nella survives and returns with new insight; Seren presents plausible warnings.
Act II: Tavi learns that cartography cannot freeze people in place; Nella and Tavi disagree but remain friends; Orin takes action rather than waiting.
Act III: Tavi chooses a network capable of change; Nella chooses independent exploration; Seren admits a decentralized alternative and participates in repairing consequences.

## Narrative delivery
- No huge unskippable opening narration. Lore found by making discoveries and speaking with people.
- Primary dialogue is concise, with optional deep branches for curious players. Dialogue may be re-read from Roadbook.
- Character conversations occur at recognizable sites and have physical reactions, little gestures and silent beats.
- Key items and road repair consequences have local visual or social payoff.
- Avoid confusing selective route-associated memory blur with literal disappearance/death of people.
- Side quests may change epilogue vignette combinations, not major ending canon.

## Ending details
Original Thimblebrook theme returns with orchestrated motifs from regions. Repaired bridges, new merchant stall and travelers communicate a changed but living home. Tavi opens completed Roadbook; wind turns to a blank page beyond known world. No post-credits plot twist undermining the closure. Postgame access can return player to exploration after epilogue without rewriting consequences.

## Writing quality checklist
Does each scene add character, world information, an emotional payoff, or a player's choice? Is the antagonist's motivation fair without excusing consequences? Is Nella autonomous? Is Orin's exit legible and non-exploitative? Does the new network solution follow established Wayline rules? Does final theme reward the opening relationships?
