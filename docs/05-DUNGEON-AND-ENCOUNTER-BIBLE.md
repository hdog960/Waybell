# WAYBELL — Dungeons, Puzzle Grammar, and Encounter Bible
Version: Phase 0 / 1.0 | Authority: all nine dungeon designs, equipment dependencies, boss patterns.
Purpose: an authoritative concept-level room sequence with named challenges; not engine geometry or an implemented floor plan.

## Universal authored dungeon structure
1. Distinct approach and ambient foreshadowing; 2. Arrival landmark and low-risk skill primer; 3. Hub or looping navigation; 4. First combined mechanic; 5. New permanent item; 6. Revisit earlier route with item; 7. Large centerpiece; 8. Late multi-rule challenge; 9. Preboss checkpoint and readable boss arena; 10. Return shortcut, story payoff and overworld implications.
Expect 75–120 min per main dungeon after balancing. No procedural room generation. Puzzle language: Observe -> Experiment -> Understand -> Combine -> Master.
Every major dungeon needs: an evocative readable silhouette; its own material/color grammar and musical signature; 2+ shortcuts to avoid busywork; 1 distinct midpoint reveal; 1 newly acquired tool; at least 2 nonmandatory side rooms; safe replay and easy recovery from failure. Disable unearned later equipment from bypassing main puzzle logic unless deliberately supported.

## Dungeon I — Rootbound Mill (Wilderbloom)
Theme: giant overgrown watermill with roots, vertical bridges and water machinery.
Prior abilities: base Echo Lantern, melee/shield. Reward: Rootspool.
R01 Forest sluice entrance — identify dry channel vs active turning wheel; safe approach.
R02 Lower mill hall — observe two water routes; first manual gate controls.
R03 Turbine floor — align water troughs; teach legible water-flow cues.
R04 Root gallery loop — navigate ledges and use Replay on a resonant plate.
R05 Millkeeper's nest — miniboss Thornhopper nest plus environmental dodge lesson.
R06 Rootspool alcove — scripted tool discovery and low-risk anchor tutorial.
R07 Upper wheel traverse — swing across formerly unreachable wooden towers, restore wheel alignment.
R08 Hidden orchard balcony — optional health reward, reveal stone inscription.
R09 Central spindle chamber — combine Echo plate + Rootspool pull to turn the largest wheel; opens boss shortcut.
R10 Briarbell arena — boss. Phase A dodge root swipes and pull shield vines, Phase B restart wheel while using Echo decoy, Phase C swing to exposed bell core during a timed but forgiving opening. Distinct weak point/cues; checkpoint at entrance.
After: Great Bell rings; water-fed bridge and forest route reopen. Return-value Rootspool anchors visible in Long Reach.
Failure guard: any swinging miss lands player safely near last platform; no indefinite wall camera occlusion.

## Optional dungeon 1 — Forgotten Tollhouse (Long Reach)
Opens after early Lantern tutorial; 20–40 min target. Three abandoned toll checkpoints connected beneath road, echo inscriptions and resonant plate sequencing. Boss: Tollkeeper Shade, telegraphed charge that follows reconstructed lines. Reward: stronger Reveal clue reception, not a main-story key. Distinct 5-beat route: entrance -> historical toll mural -> first Echo plates -> shade fight -> route restoration altar.

## Optional dungeon 2 — Hollowglass Theater (Brasshollow)
Requires Sunlens. A dark theater of rotating mirrors and recorded performers. Puzzle: align the stage lamp while Echo replicates a performer position; cues accessible visually. Boss: Stagehand Apparition with obvious curtain/sweep attack. Reward: Replay extended from six to eight seconds. Five beats: foyer -> backstage mirrors -> Echo cast rehearsal -> stage confrontation -> repaired show.

## Optional dungeon 3 — Reedcrypt (Tidemarrow)
Requires Tideweights. Submerged memorial accessible through safe buoyancy puzzles and narrative inscriptions. Boss: Reedcrypt Warden whose submerged armor requires waterline switching. Reward: wider Restore range on compatible structures. Five beats: marsh entrance -> water-step puzzle -> crypt echo stories -> warden -> reward shrine.

## Universal puzzles and QA rubric
For every authored puzzle in implementation, the level specification must record: ID, entrance and camera view, inventory preconditions, objects and visual language, observed hint, intended solution steps, expected feedback, failure/reset handling, assistance hint tiers, keyboard/touch input equivalence, save behavior, exploit tests, accessibility alternative. If a puzzle lacks a plain-language intended solution before construction, it fails preproduction readiness. Equipment mismatch should not create softlocks. Optional loops never conceal mandatory keys.

## Encounter/boss QA rubric
For each enemy and boss: silhouette; aggro distance and on-screen rules; attack telegraph frames/duration TBD in testing; distinct audio and visual warning; attack range; dodge/block/skill counter; hit feedback; arena collision; camera occlusion checks; recovery; reset on save reload; difficulty preset scaling. Boss arenas must not place touch controls over target weak points or warnings.

## Design risks
Combined tool puzzles can become arbitrary. Mitigation: single-mechanic rehearsals, readable icon grammar, intentionally modular final sequence. Height/camera ambiguity can cause frustration. Mitigation: landing silhouettes, authored camera adjustments and no blind jumps. Mobile budgets may reduce geometry complexity; preserve clarity and mood before adding rooms.
