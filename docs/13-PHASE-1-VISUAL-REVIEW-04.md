## Target gameplay camera and visual-size consistency — renewed contract
1. Normal exploration: stable elevated 3/4 view across village, grass, timber, stairs, and stone bridge. A visible ground plane and independently recognisable higher/lower walkable floors. No side-scroller or hero-centered over-the-shoulder drift.
2. Tavi scale: test approximately 10–16% of landscape frame height for broad exploration; use a nearer view temporarily when required for character expression or combat. This is a provisional composition target, not a measured hardware acceptance threshold.
3. Combat: SAME lens, camera bearing, elevation grammar, apparent Tavi scale and collision-plane perception as exploration. Only a very modest authored reframing to bring one enemy into view is acceptable; do not swap to a separate sidescrolling fight camera.
4. Foreground control: tree canopy, house roofs and hanging signage may frame a vignette but cannot obscure Tavi's feet, enemy tells, doorways or the intended route. Plan to fade occluding shapes in the engine later (not implemented).
5. Input: outer ~16% each side for left movement and right action/tool/dodge, with central ~68% protected for gameplay. Controls must be adjustable to notch/safe-area variations; all values are proposals before actual device evaluation.
6. Indication: interactables amber + icon contours; Replay cyan parallel trace; Restore violet dashed temporary structure; enemy danger RED rim and directional shape; avoid a single color as the only clue.
7. Font/UI: extremely restrained HUD at top corners; no crowded panel over scenic sightline, interactables or boss telegraph; no fake inventory/currency totals to suggest functioning gameplay.

## Dormant Rootbound Mill — first-visit law
Every first-visit shot must communicate:
- Dry or drained water intake; no flowing waterfalls into the primary wheel, no white spray.
- Main wheel stopped and visibly seized by heavy roots and a jammed hub.
- Lower turbine hall = first water gate + dry trough, readable low walkway, wall-mounted Echo plate.
- Upper spindle galleries = tiered return walkway, visible fixed anchor rings, safely outlined fall hazards.
- Tavi should be about the same perceived size as in any normal exploration room.
- Later post-repair screenshot may have strong flowing water and wheel motion as emotional payoff; it is NOT the initial state.

## Combat readability — acceptance concept
A Barkling or Thornhopper must face the player with a coherent attack windup and spatially correct feet shadow. Telegraph shape (arc / wedge / circle) must be visible on walkable ground, not afloat on a wall or beneath UI. Player maintains a visually plausible dodge lane and target remains readable at the proposed exploration zoom. Touch zones must not overlap that attack footprint. Current illustrated combat extract proves **style possibility**, not same-camera equivalence or in-game timing. A true matched scene painting is still required.
