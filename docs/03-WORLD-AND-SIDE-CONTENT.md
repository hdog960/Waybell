# WAYBELL — World, Connectivity, Quests, and Waymaking
Version: Phase 0 / 1.0 | Authority: seven region identities, geography, routes, authored optional content.

## World topology and routing
**Long Reach** is the central overland hub and contains Thimblebrook. Major spoke pathways connect to Wilderbloom (NW), Brasshollow (NE), Tidemarrow (SW), Ashglass (SE), Snowglass (N); the Broken Horizon is accessible after the penultimate mainline chapter. Visual map direction is conceptual, NOT a literal final geometry. Connect Wilderbloom to Snowglass through a late Rootspool/Galecloak short route; connect Ashglass to Tidemarrow through a post-dungeon lowland machinery cut-through. Every region needs one approach road, one unmistakable horizon landmark, two or more memorable subareas, and at least one later-return reward.

**World graph (requirements):**
- Start/Long Reach -> Wilderbloom after lantern tutorial; Rootbound Mill unlocks Rootspool.
- Long Reach -> Brasshollow after first Great Bell and Rootspool bridge/route; Bellfound Choir unlocks Sunlens and Nella rescue.
- Brasshollow completion -> Tidemarrow AND Ashglass open in either order. No mandatory Ironhand puzzle blocks Drowned Archive; no mandatory Tideweights puzzle blocks Hearth Forge. Optional cross-tool treasure okay if signposted.
- Both middle dungeons -> Act II return to Long Reach -> Snowglass. Sleeping Orrery uses all preceding tools and provides Galecloak.
- Snowglass, story/community preparation -> Broken Horizon -> Unmade Road.
- Restored Roadposts allow free, menu-based fast travel between already visited unlocked destinations, except during major encounters. No currency fee, no invented travel metrics.
- All mandatory route changes are authored and explicitly recorded in the game-state model.

## Region atlas
### 1. The Long Reach — central valley
Mood: golden meadows, intimate farm lanes, flowing streams, sunrise views. Starting village Thimblebrook, eastern river crossing, quarry path, hilltop overview, Forgotten Tollhouse. Landmark: ruined watchtower on the main ridge. Gameplay: teach pathfinding, first camera reveal, NPC routines, optional ledges and early echo puzzles. Change: new refugees, merchant stalls, repaired crossings, Orin's empty chair after chapter 8. Return tool checks: Sunlens marker beneath old tower; Rootspool on viaduct; Galecloak hill cut-through. Hostile wildlife rare near village.

### 2. Wilderbloom — ancient vertical forest
Mood: living timber, leaf-light, overgrown millwork, moss and layered canopy. Settlement Fernrest. Subareas: Fernrest boardwalks, suspended canopy, sinkroot hollows, flooded mill inlet. Landmark: gigantic split tree. Gameplay: height, redirect water, Rootspool anchor patterns. Enemies: Barklings (rush), Thornhoppers (leap), Mossbacks (territorial charge). Change: waterwheels reconnect gardens and bridges; Brother Fen's sanctuary receives travelers. Late secret: wind path to Snowglass.

### 3. Brasshollow — cliffside brass city
Mood: warm bell-metal, tile rooftops, hanging lifts, interior candlelight. Settlement Brasshollow City. Subareas: market terraces, cable-lift yards, bell cathedral, abandoned theater. Landmark: cluster of five great bell spires. Gameplay: mirror/light alignment, vertical routes, social mystery. Enemies: Brass Sentinels (shield), Mirror Wisps (blink), Watchers (ranged cue). Change: lift service, roof routes, market revitalization; Mara's repair quest.

### 4. Tidemarrow — river marsh and flooded history
Mood: silver mist, reeds, green-blue water, ruin silhouettes. Settlement Reedhaven. Subareas: reed ferry, half-sunk streets, reservoir locks, Drowned Archive and Reedcrypt. Landmark: half-submerged bell arch. Gameplay: water levels, buoyancy, underwater clear indicators; no extended forced breath timer. Enemies: Mirelings (ambush), Reed Stalkers (range), Siltbound Guardians (slow heavy). Change: ferries and fisheries resume; previously flooded home accessible.

### 5. Ashglass Highlands — basalt and the old forge
Mood: ember-red light, dense rock, stormed black hills, machinery. Settlement Emberhold. Subareas: cinder pass, cooling galleries, furnace tramway, Hearth Forge. Landmark: enormous dormant chimney. Gameplay: magnetic blocks, polarity, timed industrial movement. Enemies: Ember Crawlers (swarm), Ironbound Soldiers (armor), Furnace Wraiths (telegraphed fire). Change: lifts and cooling channels restart; Dorrik helps people relocate.

### 6. Snowglass Peaks — heights and celestial discovery
Mood: icy blues, distant clear skies, wind-chimes, starlight. Settlement Starfall Outpost. Subareas: cliff routes, crystal cavern, windbreak camp, Sleeping Orrery. Landmark: rotating observatory dome. Gameplay: wind lanes, gliding, cross-tool mastery; heights readable with projected landing indicators. Enemies: Rimehounds (flank), Gale Wraiths (wind), Crystal Guardians (reflect). Change: safe sky routes reconnect remote mountain camps.

### 7. Broken Horizon — fractured network heart
Mood: familiar places displaced into impossible sequences, glowing road segments, deep quiet. No ordinary functioning settlement. Subareas: orphaned roads, borrowed landmarks, central switching chamber, Unmade Road dungeon. Gameplay: final combinations and Echo identity. Enemy variants: Echo Phantoms and transformed previous families; avoid merely changing color. Change: postgame becomes an open memorial space with stable paths.

## NPC schedules and temporal state
Each major settlement needs a compact authored daily routine: morning activities, afternoon errands, evening gathering, night rest, with flags for region repairs and story progression. The day-night cycle affects ambience and selected interactions, not mandatory waiting. Player can wait until next morning at safe places. Story-critical people must have a fallback interaction location and map hint; never require searching blindly for an NPC. Season changes are story-authored, not a full realtime seasonal simulation.

W02 Wilderbloom Garden Channel (after Mill) -> reconnect botanical quarter.
W03 Brasshollow Upper Lift (after Bellfound) -> roof transit and vendor access.
W04 Brasshollow Bellwalk Viaduct (after Mara's quest) -> upper city loop.
W05 Tidemarrow Reed Ferry (after Archive) -> shorter marsh traversal.
W06 Tidemarrow Reservoir Gates (after Tideweights) -> optional houses emerge.
W07 Ashglass Cooling Tram (after Forge) -> forge-town safe shortcut.
W08 Snowglass Windposts (after Orrery) -> reach outer observatory lookout.
Projects use existing tool interactions and story events, not resource grind. Required projects are automatically earned through main-story progress when necessary; optional ones make traversal easier and settlements livelier.

## Secret and reward density targets (hypotheses)
Each region should show one strong far-off landmark, three recognizable mid-distance destinations, and optional discoveries roughly every few minutes of motivated traversal—subject to testing, no numeric quota that encourages filler. Landmark silhouettes, map markings and visual cues should carry navigation. No ubiquitous floating arrows or permanent dotted GPS trail.
